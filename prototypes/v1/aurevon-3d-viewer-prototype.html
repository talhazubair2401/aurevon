Test your own 3D model

Drop a .glb file onto the viewer or choose one here. The viewer swaps in your model and checks it against a suggested mobile budget. The budget numbers are a proposal for P-09, not an approved limit.

What this prototype tests

  • three.js as the 3D technology for P-09.
  • Gallery atmosphere from MTS §3.3: a floating hanger, gold reflections reserved for the 3D scene, and slow natural motion.
  • Colour variants updating the 3D garment live.
  • A static image fallback and a "Show image" alternative (§3.4, §10.2).

Built to keep shopping safe

  • The 3D viewer runs in isolation. If it fails, product info, sizes and Add to bag still work (§6.3).
  • Reduced-motion users get no auto-rotate or floating.
  • Stock is tracked per size and colour, with its own SKU (Module 02).
